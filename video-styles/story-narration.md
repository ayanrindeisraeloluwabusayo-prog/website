# Story narration (how the owner wants every script told)

From the five reference narration videos the owner sent (measured from word timings). Apply to EVERY explainer
script before showing it to the owner, and check the numbers below.

## Rules
1. **Open in the middle of a story**, in a scene, in the present tense ("He put a bronze ball in a dragon's mouth...",
   "You leave home at eight, and the drive feels like it's never going to end"). Never open with a question, a
   definition or a summary of the point.
2. **Never stop moving.** Each sentence pulls into the next with a connector: "and", "then", "but", "so", "because",
   "you see", "now", "and here's the strange part". No stand-alone fragments ("High up." "Same result.").
3. **Long, flowing sentences: 14-20 words on average**, some longer. Short lines (about 5 words) are the old
   "punchy" style the owner moved away from. One short line is allowed only to set up a turn ("And here's the
   strange part.").
4. **The surprise lands mid-flow**, inside a running sentence, with no dramatic pause before it.
5. **End on something the viewer can use or repeat**, still inside a flowing sentence.

## Voice
- The references run at 3.3-3.8 words a second with pauses under 0.3 s. For the free Kokoro voice the owner chose
  slower: **about 3.2 words a second** (speed 1.08, pauses trimmed to 0.3 s), over a minute is fine.

## Check before sending a script
- First sentence: is it a scene? Average sentence length: 14 or more? Fragments: none? Connectors between sentences?
