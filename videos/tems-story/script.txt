[0:00-0:17 Tems in her own voice, from the clip: "You guys, I didn't expect this. You know, Spotify told me they were gonna invite my fans, and I was like, 'Are you sure?' They said I don't have fans in Nigeria. So I was... I'm a bit confused."]

She really thought that. But here's the twist. Nobody in that crowd bought a ticket. Spotify invited every one of them, picked by what they actually listen to. The proof was in the data.

Which is funny, because years earlier, almost nobody wanted to hear her. "No one wanted to produce for me back then," she said. So she did it herself, hours of YouTube tutorials, learning to produce in her room.

In 2018, she quit her marketing job to release her first single, Mr. Rebel. She hadn't even finished making it yet. It didn't blow up. Then came Try Me. Then Wizkid's Essence. Then Drake. Then a song sampling her voice went to number one in America, and she walked away with a Grammy.

So when she says she's a bit confused, we get it. The fans were always there. They were just quiet, until someone counted them.
