Lagos, September. Tems is on stage in a small room, surrounded on every side by people singing her songs back to her. And she stops. "I was confused," she says. "I thought they said I didn't have fans in Nigeria." Here's the twist. Nobody in that room bought a ticket. Spotify invited every one of them, picked by what they actually listen to. The proof was in the data. Which is funny, because a few years earlier, almost nobody wanted to hear her. "No one wanted to produce for me back then," she said. So she did it herself, hours of YouTube tutorials, learning to produce in her room. In 2018, she quit her marketing job to release her first single, Mr. Rebel. She hadn't even finished making it yet. It didn't blow up. Then came Try Me. Then Wizkid's Essence. Then Drake. Then a song sampling her voice went to number one in America, and she walked away with a Grammy. So when she says she was confused, she's right to be. The fans were always there. They were just quiet, until someone counted them.
