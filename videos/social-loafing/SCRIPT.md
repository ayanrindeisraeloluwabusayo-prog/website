# Why the more people pull, the less each one pulls (social loafing)

Script style: **Full Blend**. The "star" is a historical figure (Max Ringelmann). Draft for the owner's approval.

## Script (~190 words, about 62 s)

It's a group project. Five of you. And somehow, one person ends up doing all the work.
Or, if you're honest, you're the one doing less.

Now listen to this. Over a hundred years ago, a French farm engineer called Max Ringelmann had men pull on a rope,
first alone and then in teams, and he measured every single pull.

And you see, the more men he added, the less each one pulled, until in a team of eight, each man was pulling only
about half as hard as he did on his own. Psychologists later gave it a name: social loafing.
But here's the twist: in 1979, Bibb Latane and his team put blindfolds and headphones on students, told them other
people were shouting with them, and asked them to shout as loud as they could. They were actually alone, and they
still shouted less. Nobody was there to slack off with. Just believing their effort would disappear into a group was
enough.

So Ringelmann's rope still pulls on you today. In your next group project, put a name on every task.
When everyone can see who did what, nobody hides.

## Facts and where each comes from
- Ringelmann's rope-pulling work (late 1800s, published 1913): individual effort fell as the team grew; with eight
  pullers each gave about half (49 %) of their solo effort (widely cited from Kravitz & Martin, 1986).
- Latane, B., Williams, K., & Harkins, S. (1979). Many hands make light the work: The causes and consequences of social
  loafing. JPSP, 37(6), 822-832. Clapping and shouting alone or in groups; in pseudo-groups participants were blindfolded
  and wore headphones, believed others were shouting with them, and still produced less.
- Williams, K., Harkins, S., & Latane, B. (1981). Identifiability as a deterrent to social loafing. JPSP, 40(2), 303-311:
  when each person's output could be identified, loafing disappeared (basis for the closing tip).
