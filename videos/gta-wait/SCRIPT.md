# Your brain on GTA 6, before it even came out (Full Blend, v2)

November nineteenth. Midnight. Thirteen years of waiting, and it's finally in your hands. But here's something nobody tells you: your brain was partying long before you pressed start. Remember when the first GTA 6 trailer leaked online in December 2023, and Rockstar had to drop it early? Within twenty-four hours, more than ninety million people had watched it. Ninety million brains, lighting up for a game they couldn't even play. Now, the chemical behind that is dopamine, and in the 1990s a neuroscientist called Wolfram Schultz found out what really sets it off. He gave monkeys a sip of juice right after a light or a sound, and you see, at first their dopamine fired for the juice, but after a while it fired for the signal instead. The trailer was the signal. The game was the juice. But here's the twist: when the juice they expected didn't come, their dopamine dropped below normal. Your brain doesn't reward what you get. It rewards the gap between what you expected and what you got. That's why the biggest hype can lead to the hardest crash, even when the game is great. So after thirteen years of trailers, here's how you win: stop watching the leaks. Skip the spoilers. Let the game surprise you, because a surprise is the one thing your brain can't see coming.
