# YarnGPT voice samples

Made with YarnGPT2 (Apache-2.0, by Saheed Azeez) and the WavTokenizer decoder (MIT), run locally with
`speak.py` (kept in the session scratchpad, not in the repo). Line read: "Why do you hate your own voice on a
voice note? / You press play, and a stranger answers. / Higher. Thinner. Not you. / But it is you."

Clearest: onye, osagie, jude, emma (male), chinenye (female). The others slur or skip words on this text.
Short fragments ("Higher. Thinner.") come out wrong in every voice.
