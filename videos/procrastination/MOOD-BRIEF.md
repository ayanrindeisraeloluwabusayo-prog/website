# Mood brief: "You're not lazy" (procrastination, 30 s)

Facts:
- Procrastination as short-term mood repair: Sirois & Pychyl, *Social and Personality Psychology Compass*
  7(2), 115-127 (2013).
- Future self vs other people in the brain: Ersner-Hershfield, Wimmer & Knutson, *Social Cognitive and
  Affective Neuroscience* 4(1), 85-92 (2009), Stanford University. In the scanner, thinking about one's
  future self looked less like thinking about oneself and more like thinking about another person.
