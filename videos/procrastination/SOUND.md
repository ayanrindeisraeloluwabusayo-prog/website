# Sound plan: "You're not lazy"

Effects are made by `make_sfx.py` (`python3 make_sfx.py`, needs scipy). A sound plays only where
something happens on screen. The music dips under "lazy.", the dread drop and "smaller.". The final mix
is at -14 LUFS.
