# Episode 1 preview: Two Hours at the Bus Stop (mini story, invented)

He sat at the bus stop for two whole hours. Bus after bus drove right past him. Not one of them stopped. So he started
shouting at every driver. Then an old woman walking by looked at him and said: "Son, you have to wave your hand."
He had been sitting there for two hours, and he never once waved. Sometimes nothing changes, because you never asked.
