The `library/` files are the `design-md/<name>/DESIGN.md` files from
https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md (commit fcc7028), MIT licensed, renamed to
`<name>.md`. They are the same files published on https://getdesign.md and listed by
https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-claude-design. `INDEX.md` is generated from each file's
`description` field (or its first paragraph).

The files are unofficial analyses of public websites; the brands, logos and proprietary fonts
they describe belong to their owners.

To update: re-clone that repository, copy each `design-md/<name>/DESIGN.md` to
`library/<name>.md`, and regenerate `INDEX.md`.
